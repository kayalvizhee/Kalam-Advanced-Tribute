01 — THE MAN BEHIND THE MISSION

Greatness begins
with humility.

Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, on 15 October 1931, Abdul Kalam rose from a modest background to become one of India's most respected scientists and leaders.

His contributions to space research and missile development helped advance India's scientific capabilities. Yet he was equally admired for his simplicity, kindness and extraordinary connection with students.

To millions, he was more than a President. He was a teacher who believed that the future of a nation rests in the minds of its young people.

02 — THE JOURNEY

Every milestone tells
a story of purpose.

Explore the defining moments of an extraordinary life.

1931
THE BEGINNING

Roots in Rameswaram

Born on 15 October in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, where his journey of curiosity and determination began.

1950s
EDUCATION

A passion for aerospace

Studied physics before specialising in aeronautical engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology.

1980
SPACE RESEARCH

A milestone in Indian spaceflight

As project director of SLV-III, he helped lead the successful launch that placed the Rohini satellite in orbit.

1980s–90s
NATIONAL SERVICE

Advancing defence technology

Played a significant role in India's guided missile development and strategic technology programmes.

1997
RECOGNITION

Honoured with the Bharat Ratna

Received India's highest civilian award in recognition of his contributions to science and public service.

2002
LEADERSHIP

President of India

Became the 11th President of India and inspired young citizens through his approachable leadership.

2015
A LIVING LEGACY

Teaching until the end

Passed away on 27 July while addressing students at IIM Shillong, remaining devoted to education until the end.
